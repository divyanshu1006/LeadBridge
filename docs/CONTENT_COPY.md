## 4. HOW IT WORKS PAGE

### 4.1 Hero

**Headline:**  
## From Zero Outreach to Qualified Meetings in 30 Days

**Subheadline:**  
Our proven 5-step process is designed to start generating buyer meetings within your first month.

### 4.2 Process Steps

**Step 1: Discovery Call (Day 1)**
> We start with a 30-minute call to understand your business. What do you manufacture? Who's your ideal buyer? What's your current sales process? This call shapes everything that follows.

**Step 2: Lead Research (Day 2-7)**
> Based on our discovery call, we research and build a targeted prospect list. We identify companies that need your products, find the decision-makers, and verify their contact information.

**Step 3: Campaign Setup (Day 7-10)**
> We craft personalized outreach messages, set up email sequences, prepare LinkedIn connection strategies, and build WhatsApp messaging templates — all tailored to your industry and ideal buyer.

**Step 4: Outreach Launch (Day 10+)**
> We launch your outreach campaign across all channels. We monitor responses in real-time, handle replies, qualify interested prospects, and continuously optimize our messaging based on results.

**Step 5: Meetings Booked (Day 15+)**
> When a qualified buyer is interested, we book the meeting directly on your calendar. You get a pre-meeting brief with everything you need to walk in confident and close.
